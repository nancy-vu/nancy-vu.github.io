#### Introduction

While studying in school, I contributed to many pieces for the school newsletter which reflect my growth as an academic and a person throughout the years. Some of the pieces I wrote then I do not wholly subscribe to anymore, especially my earliest pieces where I had just begun to think about intersectionality and representation. I believe that my ideas are more fluid these days, while still trying to seek that critical comparative perspective I was beginning to develop. At the same time, though I wrote these pieces upwards of 2-3 years ago, many of them still resonate with me and who I aim to become as a person and as a community member to all those around me. I hope that they can contribute to fruitful conversations. All my publications can be accessed on the official Swarthmore Voices website.
